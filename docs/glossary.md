# Glossary

The words this kit uses, in the order you will meet them.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Game Master (GM)** | The host. Hands out envelopes, keeps time, helps people find each other, and gives the conclusion. Plays a non-acting "Family Solicitor" and is not a suspect |
| **Character** | A role a guest plays, such as Inspector Donna Reed. 52 players, each with a pre-party briefing, dress guidance and a secret |
| **Performer** | A player with a short acting moment: Vincent/Peter Vale, DJ Spinny T, Inspector Donna Reed, the murderer, Leo Lights, Wanda Wires, Scooter, Dakota Steele and Vance Vellum |
| **Briefing** | What a guest is sent before the party: who they are, what they want, how to dress. Stored in `characters.description` |
| **Card** (clue, clue record) | One short message to one player in one round. 207 are handed to guests; a few are private to performers or the Game Master |
| **Round 0 to 4** | Round 0 is the arrival reception and the staged murder; Rounds 1 to 3 are the investigation; Round 4 is the closing sequence |
| **Envelope** | Each player's cards for a round travel in their own envelope. Round 1 envelopes sit at the place settings; Rounds 2 and 3 arrive in a table envelope |
| **Network** (clue network) | Who each card sends its player to. The network is the game: facts travel because people have a reason to talk |
| **Target** | The named person a card sends its player to |
| **Question note** | A pre-printed slip for a conversation with someone at another table: a draft question, and a blank reply |
| **Handshake** | A place where one card relies on another character having something to say back (an introduction, a mention, a question). The review lists those that have not been read lately |
| **Answer contract** | A record of where the answer to a question card lives: which source card, available by which round |
| **Case pillar** | One of the seven facts the conclusion rests on (motive, means, opportunity, forensic, arrangement, payment purpose, elimination). The review checks each table can reach them |
| **Pack** | A set of items you choose in the Print Studio and download as one PDF |
| **Snapshot** | The saved copy of the database that the Print Studio prints from (`print-studio/lib/event.json`). Refresh it with `npm run snapshot` after any change |
| **Roster** | Who plays whom and where they sit. Ships anonymous ("Guest 01"); import your own with `scripts/roster.py` |
| **Sample plan** | The shipped seating: eight tables, 54 seats, tested with the clue network. You change who plays each character, not where the characters sit |
| **Canon** | The facts of the case that every card must keep true; see [../story/solution-and-canon.md](../story/solution-and-canon.md) |
| **Placeholder** | A labelled stand-in for a picture you have not supplied; it prints its own brief |
