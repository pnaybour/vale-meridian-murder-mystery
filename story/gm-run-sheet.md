# Vale Meridian — current GM running order

Host-only instructions; contains spoilers.

Facilitates the evening, hands out clues and a small number of evidence items, explains the procedure, helps introductions and manages practical staging. Guests discover and discuss the evidence. Gives the public conclusion in Round 4 after the tables have written their conclusions; does not supply investigative testimony during earlier rounds. Not a suspect.

Before guests arrive, sort ordinary cards, shared exhibits and private performance cards by the release sequence below. Give a short welcome explaining that guests play their characters, use the facts on released cards and talk to the named people or follow other interesting leads. Personal notes are optional. Fictional times and sightlines describe the story; guests need not reproduce them in the venue.

The card's footer shows the note's key (for example "Note Q2-06"); the note shows the same key, who it is to and from, with tables, a short draft question in the asker's voice, two lines to add or change it, and a blank Reply section. Hand each guest their notes with that round's cards (they print in key order, table by table). The guest adjusts the draft if they like and sends it to the named table; the reply comes back on the same slip. Guests can still get up and talk instead. Same-table conversations and Round 0 have no notes. Twelve blank private notes remain for anything else.

## Conversations and closing

Help people locate the named person if needed. A short introduction is enough. Do not gather seven contacts, assign listeners, carry required messages or make Donna certify delivery. Let the room talk, then move to the closing round after reasonable discussion time. Do not invite final questions of named witnesses: with fifty-four guests that alone could run for hours, and the evidence has already been in the room for three rounds. The first public evidence explanation by the GM is the retained conclusion, given once the tables have discussed and each guest has written their own answer. Nobody votes and no slips are collected. Keep Veronica’s monologue, Donna’s arrest and the epilogue withheld until their individual cues.

Nobody has to perform the confession: offer to narrate it. The staged arrest involves no physical restraint. Follow the retained music cues and return promptly to the party.
