# Vale Meridian — Round 0: DJ Spinny T's running order

**For DJ Spinny T, table 7.** Everything you do in Round 0, in order: the arrival,
the people you meet, and the one music cue at the toast. Round 4 has its own sheet.

Set on the night of 10 October 1986

---

## Before guests arrive

- **Tracks ready:** *Died in Your Arms Tonight* cued for the toast. Have the Round 4 tracks loaded too
  (*It Had To Be You (Dark Version)*, *Lux Aeterna*, *Killer Queen*, *Summer of '69*), but nothing
  else from them is needed tonight until Round 4.
- **Lights:** walk the room with Leo Lights and Wanda Wires. Agree which
  stage and disco lights go off at your cue. **The house lights stay on throughout.**
- **The signal:** agree with the GM exactly what the "go" signal for the toast cue will be.

---

## The sequence

### 1 · Arrival

The GM gives you your **Round 0 clue envelope**. Inside are your card and a **deep-red envelope**
holding cash and a typed slip: *"At the toast cue, raise the music for twenty seconds. No delays."*

**Keep the deep-red envelope and slip with you all evening.** In Round 3 you compare them with
Dakota Steele's.

### 2 · Mingling

Play background music, low enough for conversation.

Your card sends you to find, in this order:

1. **Leo Lights**  and **Wanda Wires** . Tell them to follow your lead:
   when your music rises, they switch off some of the stage lights. The house lights stay on.
2. **Nikki Neon**  wants her songs played. **Scooter**  knows everybody's
   business.

Stay close enough to your desk to be back at it before the toast.

### 3 · Just before the toast

The GM hands you a **private toast card**. Go back to your desk and wait for the agreed signal.

### 4 · The toast cue ★

**On the GM's signal:** bring in *Died in Your Arms Tonight* **slightly early** and raise it enough
to turn the room towards the stage. Leo and Wanda drop some stage lights as it rises.

**Hold it about twenty seconds**, then **fade it cleanly**. It should look like an over-eager show
cue, not a blackout.

### 5 · The toast and the collapse

Vincent Vale  waits for your music to subside, then gives his toast, drinks from his
private decanter and collapses.

**No music** during the speech or the collapse. Stay at your desk and let the moment play out.

### 6 · Round 1 begins

Everyone goes through to the eating area. Your **Round 1 clue envelope** is waiting at your place
setting on table 7; open it when the GM announces Round 1.
From here you are a guest with clues like everyone else, and background music is up to you until
Round 4.
