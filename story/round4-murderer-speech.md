# Veronica Vane

Read this slowly. Short lines. Take a breath at each gap.

You have not seen this before and nobody expects you to have prepared. If you would rather not
read it aloud, hand it straight back and it will be read for you.

---

"Vince made control sound like a favour. A part. An introduction. A chance to begin again.

I was eighteen when I signed. Half of everything I have earned since has been his.

Tonight was meant to be mine. He was going to stand up and make it his.

*(pause)*

I asked Beverly for the sample. I told her Vince wanted to look at it. Scooter brought it to me,
sealed.

During the music, I went to his decanter. Nobody was watching. It took seconds.

*(pause)*

Beverly's dish never reached him. That one was hers. This one was mine.

I thought that if his hold on me ended, I would have my life back.

Instead I have brought all of us here."
