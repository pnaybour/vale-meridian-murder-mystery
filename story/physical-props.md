# Vale Meridian — physical preparation checklist

Reviewed at publication. GM copy; contains story details.

Checked against the active clue records in the event database and the GM staging instructions in story/. Photographs and printed evidence are produced by Print Studio; pictures of people are placeholders until you add your own (docs/evidence-photos.md).

Preparation checklist only: nothing on it is bought, made or checked for you. Printed evidence and stationery are generated in Print Studio; the physical props, the decanter and the envelopes are yours to gather.

## Toast and event setup

### [ ] Distinctive private decanter with removable stopper — Required

Quantity: 1 · Responsible: GM / Vincent · Ready: Before arrival

Set on a small private drinks table or tray, visibly separate from general champagne service. The stopper matters to several witness accounts. Agree the location with Vincent, Dakota and Veronica (she must be able to reach it unseen).

Clue references: CLUE_VV_R0, CLUE047, CLUE_VV_R1, CLUE_R3_DAKOTA_01, CLUE_VVL_R3

### [ ] Red wine or a suitable red grape-juice substitute — Required

Quantity: Enough for the rehearsed pour and toast · Responsible: GM / Vincent · Ready: Before the toast

Use an ordinary drink agreed with the performer in the private decanter. The decanter and the red wine are real and Vale drinks from them during the toast. Serve the guests champagne, so that Vale is visibly the only person drinking red: that is what puts Lucien's Round 1 observation in front of the whole room rather than leaving it on a card. Nothing is added to the real drink.

Clue references: CLUE_VV_R0, CLUE_DONNA_R1, CLUE_VV_R2

### [ ] Vincent’s own drinking glass — Required

Quantity: 1, plus a practical spare · Responsible: GM / Vincent · Ready: Before the toast

Clue references: CLUE_VV_R0, CLUE_DONNA_R1, CLUE_VV_R2

### [ ] Name badges, holders and seating stationery — Required

Quantity: 55 badges; 54 holders; 54 place cards; 8 table signs · Responsible: GM · Ready: Before arrival

Badges are 90 × 56 mm with crop marks, eight per A4 sheet. One performer swaps Vincent for Peter, so two badges are printed for that seat but 54 holders suffice; Peter’s badge prints on its own private sheet. Use holders or clips that fit the finished badge size. Counts come from the approved seating plan.

Clue references: CLUE_VV_R0, CLUE170

### [ ] Clue envelopes and their labels — Required

Quantity: 207 envelopes; 207 labels on 12 Avery J8161 sheets · Responsible: GM · Ready: Before arrival, sorted by round

Every character gets their own envelope for each round: 51 in Round 0 and 52 in each of Rounds 1 to 3. Print the labels from Print Studio (Voting & notes) on Avery J8161 or L7161, 63.5 × 46.6 mm, eighteen per sheet. Each round starts a fresh sheet, so the sorting stays honest and the part-used sheet at the end of a round leaves spare labels for anything written by hand. Each label carries the guest name to read across a table, the character underneath to confirm the right card, and the table number. Envelopes need to take a 186 × 130 mm card, so C5 or a similar size.

### [ ] Voting slips and pens — Required

Quantity: 56 slips; about 1 pen per attendee plus spares · Responsible: GM · Ready: Ready before Round 4

Tables use the slips while they discuss who committed the murder: one accused name, with the motive, means and opportunity. Nobody collects them. Pen quantities are a practical host suggestion.

### [ ] Private note sheets and spare plain paper — Required

The print app supplies To, From and a lined Message area, two A5 notes per landscape A4 sheet. Writing a note remains optional; guests can speak directly.
