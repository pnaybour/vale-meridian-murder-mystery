## How the name works

Wherever the murderer's name belongs, this script says **[THE NAME]**.

- **The GM** knows it, and the GM's copy has it written in.
- **Donna** does not need it in advance. The GM says it aloud seconds before her line, and she
  repeats it.
- **Spinny** never needs it at all.

So the three of you can rehearse this in full, beforehand, without two of you learning who did it.

**Hand this out at the start of Round 4.** One each. Keep the speech folded until step 6.

## The sequence

### 1 · The GM opens the closing round

**GM says:**

> "You have heard the witnesses. Talk it over at your table, then each write down who you think
> did it and the evidence that persuaded you."

**Spinny — cue 1.** On the words **"You have heard the witnesses"**, bring in
*It Had To Be You (Dark Version)* quietly. Well under the GM's voice. Texture, not foreground.

### 2 · Table discussion and written conclusions

Each table talks it over, and every guest writes their own conclusion on an accusation slip.
Nobody votes and nothing is collected or counted: the slips are each person's own answer, to
compare with the conclusion when it comes. This takes as long as it takes.

**Spinny — hold.** *It Had To Be You* plays all the way through underneath the talking. **Let it
run; do not restart it.** The discussion lasts as long as it lasts; the track is the bed under it,
not a timer.

### 3 · Pens down — the turn into the conclusion

**GM says:**

> "Pens down, please."

**Spinny — cue 2.** On **"Pens down"**, bring *It Had To Be You* to a close and **start**
*Lux Aeterna* **on loop**. Bring it up only as far as it can go while the GM stays comfortably
audible. It plays from here to the arrest, so set it looping and forget the running time: the
only thing that ends it is cue 3.

**GM — come in over the music.** *Lux Aeterna* runs for twenty seconds or so while the room
finishes writing and settles, then announce the elimination over it. Nothing is being held for
effect: the music starts, and you speak once it is up.

### 4 · The standing elimination

<!-- SHARED -->
**GM:** runs the standing elimination from the tables. Everybody stands where they are sitting,
and people sit again as each test rules them out; nobody moves table. The GM's copy has the
words. Nobody is out of the game, and anyone who would rather stay seated may.
<!-- /SHARED -->
<!-- GM -->
**GM says:**

> "Would everybody please stand, just where you are. Nobody is out of the game — and if you would rather stay seated, do.
>
> Motive. Vale owned people. Sit down if your character had no reason to want him gone.
>
> Means. He died of a stimulant overdose, from a sample in a sealed silver compact. Sit down if you never had access to it.
>
> Opportunity. It went into his private decanter during the twenty seconds of music. Stay standing only if you handled that decanter, or could reach it."

Several people will still be standing, not just Veronica. That is fine: the case narrows it, not the room.
<!-- /GM -->

**Spinny — hold.** *Lux Aeterna* low. Everyone is at their own table, as on the table plan, and
stands and sits in place; nobody moves about the room. It stays quieter than it looks, so do not
lift the music to compete with it.

**Donna — join in.** Stand and sit with everyone else as the tests apply to you. Nothing else until step 7.

### 5 · The case, and the name

<!-- SHARED -->
**GM:** sets out the case to those still standing. The GM's copy has it in full. Then:

> "The murderer is **[THE NAME]**."
<!-- /SHARED -->
<!-- GM -->
**GM says**, to those still standing. Unhurried: about a minute and a half. Each **Look at** line tells you who to find as you mention them. Do not look at Veronica until the last line.

**Look at:** Tommy Tech (table 2) · Sabrina Sinclair (table 3)

**Look at:** Dr. Beverly Steele (table 4) · Scooter (table 3)

**Look at:** Leo Lights (table 7) · Glenda Gold (table 1) · Dakota Steele (table 7)

> "Then she bought herself twenty seconds. Leo Lights watched her leave a deep-red envelope on Spinny's desk: 'At the toast cue, raise the music for twenty seconds. No delays.' **Look at:** Nikki Neon (table 5) · Dakota (table 7) · Ronnie Reels (table 2)

**Look at:** Veronica Vane (table 1). Now, and not before.

> "Motive, means and opportunity. One person had all three. The murderer is the comeback girl — **Veronica Vane**."

### 6 · The speech

<!-- SHARED -->
**GM:** hand over the speech and **offer to read it aloud instead**. Whoever receives it has never
seen it, has had no rehearsal, and does not know until this moment. At the slightest hesitation,
read it yourself and let them listen.
<!-- /SHARED -->
<!-- GM -->
**GM:** hand the folded speech to **Veronica's player** and say:

> "Would you like to read this, or shall I read it for you?"

She has never seen it and has had no rehearsal, though she has known since Round 0 what she did. At the slightest hesitation, read it yourself and let her listen.
<!-- /GM -->

**Spinny — hold.** Keep *Lux Aeterna* low beneath it. Resist lifting it.

**Donna — stand by.** You are not waiting for a pause. When the speech ends the GM will say
**"Inspector, would you arrest them, and take them to the cells."** That sentence is your cue,
and Spinny's warning.

### 7 · The arrest ★

**GM:**

<!-- SHARED -->

<!-- /SHARED -->

> "Inspector, would you arrest her, and take her to the cells."

**Donna — step forward.** Composed, unhurried, final.

> *Optional lead-in:* "This matter is concluded."
>
<!-- SHARED -->
> **"[THE NAME], you are under arrest for the murder of Vincent Vale. You will come with me."**
<!-- /SHARED -->
<!-- GM -->
> **"Veronica Vane, you are under arrest for the murder of Vincent Vale. You will come with me."**

**Spinny — cue 3.** On the words **"you are under arrest"** — **cut** *Lux Aeterna* dead and
**slam into Killer Queen at 0:29**, so it lands on **"She's a killer queen"**. Loud. Clean start,
no fade in, no intro. This is the release the whole evening has been holding back; let the room
react.

**Donna — the exit.** Invite them to walk out with you. **No physical restraint of any kind.** A
hand towards the door is enough. Let the applause happen.

**Donna — come back in.** Once you are both through the door, wait a moment, then walk back in together as *Killer Queen* fades. You are both in the credits, and both take a bow.

### 8 · The credits

**Spinny — fade.** After the first chorus of *Killer Queen*, bring it down. The credits run a little longer now: keep it low and steady.

**GM — read the credits.** Wait until Donna and the arrested guest are back in the room. Then unhurried, one name at a time, with applause between each.

**The first three bows — rehearsed.** The credits open with Donna, the arrested guest and Spinny. When your name is read, take a step forward, bow, and hold it for the applause. Everyone named after you will copy what you do, so make it clear and unhurried.

## Spinny's cue card

Four moves. Every one is triggered by a spoken word, not by a nod — including the last.

| # | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You have heard the witnesses" | *It Had To Be You (Dark Version)* in, quiet |
| — | discussion and written conclusions | **let it play through — never restart it** |
| 2 | "Pens down" | end *It Had To Be You* · **start** *Lux Aeterna* **on loop**, low |
| — | elimination, case and speech | **hold low — speech must stay audible** |

## What to rehearse

Worth practising:

- **The first three bows.** Donna bows, then brings the arrested guest forward; Spinny stands and bows at the desk. The rest of the room copies whatever these three do. Rehearse Donna's with a stand-in, since the real guest will not know until the night.

- **Cue 3.** The cut on "you are under arrest" is the one beat that has to land exactly. Run it
  until Spinny is cutting *on* the word rather than after it.
- **The handover.** "Inspector, would you arrest them…" → Donna's line → the cut. Three lines in a
  row from three people. Run them together a few times.
- **Cue 3 landing on the words.** *Killer Queen* starts at 0:29 so the first thing the room hears
  is "She's a killer queen". Check the start point against the file you will actually play:
  pressings and remasters differ by a second or two, and a second late loses the line.
- **The *Lux Aeterna* level.** It loops under the elimination, the case and the speech, so the
  length looks after itself; the level does not. Too loud and the case is lost, too quiet and the
  arrest has nothing to cut away from.

Not worth rehearsing:

- The discussion and the writing. Unpredictable length by design.
- The name. Donna hears it from the GM seconds before she needs it.
