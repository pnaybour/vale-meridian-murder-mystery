# Picture briefs

Generated by `scripts/export_story_reader.py` from the `local_evidence_photos` table. Each entry says what one picture must show so it works in the game. Each is written as a prompt: paste it into an AI image tool such as ChatGPT (the original pictures were made this way), with Claude or another assistant to adapt it to your cast and refine the result, then save the picture in `data/artwork/` under the file name given, and run `npm run photos` in `print-studio/`. See [../docs/evidence-photos.md](../docs/evidence-photos.md) for the rules that apply to the whole set.

## The decanter photograph

- **File:** `data/artwork/decanter-frame.png` (about 1132 × 1389 px)
- **Id:** `photo-decanter` · **Round:** 2 · **Held by:** Vance Vellum · **Prints:** 9
- **Caption printed with it:** Vance Vellum · 10 October 1986, 19:29:10 · a woman from behind, her hand near the stopper. The frame does not show anything entering the wine.
- **When:** Round 2, in the pack

Vance's decanter frame, the single most useful picture in the game. A woman seen from behind at the private drinks table, her hand on the stopper, a filled glass beside her; her face is completely hidden and nothing is shown entering the wine, so it places somebody there without naming them. Black and white on purpose. What can be recognised is long blonde hair, a dark draped gown with a line of pearls along the shoulder, and a wide pearl cuff on the reaching wrist: enough for Nikki and Dakota to stand by their Round 3 identifications, not enough to settle it alone. The figure's hair, gown and bracelet are what the witnesses recognise, so keep them consistent with the murderer's costume, which should include a wide cuff bracelet on the right wrist. Nine copies print, so Vance can leave one at every table and keep his own. Released in Round 2 inside the pack, never on its own; Nikki and Dakota recognise the woman in Round 3.

## Signing in the lobby

- **File:** `data/artwork/lobby-signing-1928.png` (about 1132 × 1389 px)
- **Id:** `photo-lobby-signing` · **Round:** 2 · **Held by:** Vance Vellum · **Prints:** 9
- **Caption printed with it:** Vance Vellum · written on the print: 10·10·86  7:28 pm  V.V.
- **When:** Round 2, in the pack

A young star in a pale cream suit signing an autograph in the lobby, head bent right down over the book so his face is not visible. The hidden face is deliberate: this is the only print whose subject is also a suspect, so the picture corroborates without doing the witness's job. Johnny's card is what names Robbie and places him there from 19:28 until the alarm. The time written on the border in Vance's hand also shows that he dates his frames, which matters when the decanter picture is questioned. The fan is a dark-haired woman in her fifties in red, chosen deliberately: an earlier version gave her long blonde hair and a dark dress, which inside the same pack read as the decanter woman one minute earlier and would have handed Veronica a false alibi.
